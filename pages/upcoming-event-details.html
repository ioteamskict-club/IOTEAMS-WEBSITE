About the program

Under MOSTI's STI 100³ Programme, with the Malaysia Board of Technologists (MBOT), IoTeams Discovery: Learn, Create & Pitch 2026 brings 30 Form Four students from Tunku Kurshiah College to the Kulliyyah of Information and Communication Technology, IIUM. Their guides are our own final-year Computer Science students, who become mentors and share seven Final Year Projects in Internet of Things and artificial intelligence.

The programme is built on a simple belief: we learn best when we teach, and we understand best when we explain. Our mentors learn to explain technology in plain language, and the students learn to stand before a panel with ideas of their own.

At IoTeams, we believe proses itu jalan: the process is the path. We look forward to sharing this journey with you.
