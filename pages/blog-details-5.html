IoTeams Featured on Brunei Television at GIC 2026

IoTeams was featured on Brunei Television at the Global Innovation Challenge 2026.

▶ Watch the RTB Interview Read the story
Muhammad Nabil Zihni Bin Omar Shukri
Club member · IoTeams IIUM
📺 Featured on RTB

Our innovation for smarter, more sustainable food production was showcased to a national audience by Radio Television Brunei (RTB).

A Meaningful Milestone

Alhamdulillah, IoTeams, representing the Kulliyyah of Information and Communication Technology (KICT), International Islamic University Malaysia (IIUM), took part in the Global Innovation Challenge (GIC) 2026 at Universiti Brunei Darussalam (UBD), where our team presented its work on smarter and more sustainable food production.

During the event, Radio Television Brunei (RTB) invited us for an interview, giving us the chance to share our research and innovation with a much wider audience across Brunei and the region.

“Technology should serve people, the community and the planet.” — IoTeams
