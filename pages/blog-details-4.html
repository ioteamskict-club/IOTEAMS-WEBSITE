ALHAMDULILLAH! IoTeams V5 Secures 1st Runner-Up at Global Innovators Challenge 2026 in Brunei

From Malaysia to Brunei, our journey of innovation continues! IoTeams V5 from KICT, IIUM achieves international acclaim at Universiti Brunei Darussalam (UBD), taking home the 1st Runner-Up Researcher Innovation Award and BND 1,200 cash prize.

Ainin
IoTeams IIUM
🏆 1st Runner-Up

ALHAMDULILLAH! Another international milestone for IoTeams V5! From Malaysia to Brunei, our journey of tech innovation and student mentorship reaches new heights on the global stage.

IoTeams V5, representing the Kulliyyah of Information and Communication Technology (KICT), International Islamic University Malaysia (IIUM), has officially been awarded 1st Runner-Up in the Researcher Innovation Award category at the Global Innovators Challenge (GIC) 2026, hosted by Universiti Brunei Darussalam (UBD).

Achievement 1st Runner-Up
Event GIC 2026
Host UBD, Brunei
Cash Prize BND 1,200

At a Glance: Learn more about the key highlights, meet the IoTeams V5 squad, or read our heartfelt acknowledgments.

Beyond the Trophy

This achievement is more than a trophy. It represents the perseverance, discipline, teamwork, and countless learning experiences that shape our students into future tech innovators and leaders.

At IoTeams, we don’t simply celebrate what our students achieve. We celebrate who they become throughout the journey. Competing internationally requires balancing hardware engineering, software development, pitch preparation, and rigorous research presentation under high-pressure conditions.

"The greatest achievement is not what we win, but what we inspire others to become."

Forged in Process, Defined by Legacy

Every line of code written, sensor calibrated, and rehearsal session conducted brought IoTeams V5 closer to this benchmark. The journey from proposal submission to standing on stage at Universiti Brunei Darussalam demonstrates the standard of technical excellence nurtured within KICT.

  • Learn — Rigorous theoretical research combined with hands-on hardware development.
  • Build — Rapid prototyping and deploying scalable IoT software solutions.
  • Contribute — Representing IIUM and solving real-world technological challenges.
  • Pass It Forward — Mentoring the next generation of IoTeams innovators.

Acknowledgments & Gratitude

Our heartfelt appreciation goes to the KICT management, IIUM, our project collaborators, advisors, and everyone who supported and believed in our students throughout this journey.

To our future IoTeams generations: may this achievement inspire you to go further, contribute more, and continue the legacy. 🤲 Alhamdulillah & Jazakumullahu Khairan!
